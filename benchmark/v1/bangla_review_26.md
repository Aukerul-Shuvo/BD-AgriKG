# Bangla review of the 26 benchmark templates (v1, as evaluated)

One example per template. The Bangla is the sentence the models were given.
Fill the three lines under each one. Scales: Fluency 1 unnatural, 2 acceptable,
3 natural. Adequacy: yes, or no and what differs from the English.
District names, years and pest names stay in Latin script on purpose; crop
names must be Bangla. Nothing here changes the benchmark; corrections go to
the paper's appendix and to the corrected release.
