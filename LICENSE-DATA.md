## Sources

The data is derived from public publications of the Government of
Bangladesh and of OCHA, which remain under their publishers' terms:

- Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), Yearbook of Agricultural
  Statistics, editions 2016 to 2025 (production panel; crop calendar)
- Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC), crop suitability
  zoning (land suitability)
- Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Service (BAMIS),
  crop-meteorology thresholds
- Soil Resource Development Institute (SRDI), soil association tables
- OCHA Common Operational Datasets, Bangladesh administrative boundaries
  (COD-AB), CC BY-IGO: the P-code gazetteer
- Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE) and peer-reviewed sources
  cited row by row in `data/raw/crop_pest_corrected.csv`

The yearbook PDFs are not redistributed; `data/bbs_yearbook_urls.md` gives
their download locations.
